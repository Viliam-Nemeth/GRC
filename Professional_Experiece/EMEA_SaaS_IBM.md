# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Experience

## Overview
This document serves as a showcase of GRC I applied in my job at IBM.
As SaaS Business Process SME I had multiple contribution to improving and maintaining the correctness and auditability of our teams.

## Key Contributions

### Preventive Controls – SaaS Deal Validation
- Implemented and automated L0 validation checks
- Reduced order configuration human errors by 70%
- Ensured compliance of deals with pricing and configuration standards

### Risk Mitigation – Billing and Revenue Accuracy
- Acted as escalation point for billing discrepancies
- Identified root causes and ensured corrective actions
- Mitigated risks related to revenue leakage

### Governance – Process Integration (Post-Acquisition)
- Supported alignment of operational processes across IBM and acquired entities
- Reduced inconsistencies and improved compliance across organizations

### Transition Governance – Europe to India
- Led structured knowledge transfer and process handover
- Mitigated operational risks during organizational transition
- Ensured continuity and adherence to established standards

### Capability Governance – Training Programs
- Designed process management training programs
- Standardized execution practices across teams (Business, Sales, Acquired companies)
- Reduced delivery risk through capability building
